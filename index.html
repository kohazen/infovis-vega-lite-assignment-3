Visualizing Office Supply Sales

This multiview dashboard uses the SalesOrders data from HW_III.xlsx to compare sales reps, track product revenue over time, inspect the distribution of unit counts, and explore how regional performance and order-level sales vary across the catalog.

Time span January 2021 to December 2022
Orders 43 sales records
Interactions Region click filter and scatter brush

Required views: donut chart, streamgraph, trellis histogram with mean overlay. Extra views: interactive regional ranking and interactive order scatter plot.
